## Research question

Sparse and highly selective neurons are difficult to characterize because most sampled stimuli produce little or no response. How can a closed-loop system choose more informative stimuli and avoid spending experiments in zero-response regions?

## Approach

- Constructed a virtual neural-response testbed based on the Maximum Entropy Principle to simulate sparse, specific encoding in macaque anterior inferotemporal (AIT) cortex.
- Developed a GPU-accelerated closed-loop active-learning pipeline for adaptive stimulus sampling.
- Formulated sampling as a Markov Decision Process and explored DQN and PPO policies for retrieving rare high-response features.

## Research context

This project is supervised by **Prof. Shiming Tang** at Peking University. It brings together experimental design, computational neuroscience, and reinforcement learning.

## Methods & tools

- Python and PyTorch
