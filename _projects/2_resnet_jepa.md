## Research question

Can predictive coding — the idea that the brain continually anticipates incoming sensory input — provide a useful lens for designing and interpreting self-supervised visual learning systems?

## Approach

- Redesigned a standard CNN-JEPA framework around a ResNet backbone.
- Framed latent-space visual pre-training as a biological predictive process rather than direct pixel reconstruction.
- Investigated how predictive dynamics across the network relate to hierarchical representations in the primate ventral visual stream.

## Research context

This project is supervised by **Prof. Shiming Tang** at Peking University and sits at the intersection of computational neuroscience, computer vision, and representation learning.
