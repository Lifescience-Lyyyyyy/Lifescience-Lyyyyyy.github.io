## Research question

How can an equivariant graph neural network encode expressive higher-degree geometric features without giving up the computational efficiency of scalarization-based models?

## Approach

- Studied the design of GotenNet and established an EGNN baseline for 3D molecular property prediction.
- Developed **pro-EGNN**, combining scalarization-based computation with ideas from higher-degree steerable models.
- Evaluated the approach against the original EGNN on the QM9 dataset.
