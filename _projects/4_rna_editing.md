## Research focus

Programmable RNA editing offers a reversible route to modifying gene expression. This work explored how suppressor tRNA biology and tRNA engineering could contribute to the RESTART RNA-editing system.

## Contributions

- Participated in efforts to improve the RESTART RNA-editing system.
- Explored suppressor tRNA biology.
- Engineered tRNAs with natural and artificial modifications.

## Research context

This project was supervised by **Prof. Chengqi Yi** in the Yi Lab at Peking University.
