## Research focus

TCP transcription factors play broad roles in plant development. This early research experience examined how members of the TCP gene family contribute to leaf development in _Arabidopsis thaliana_ and soybean.

## Approach

- Constructed and studied multiple TCP-gene mutants in _Arabidopsis_ and soybean.
- Investigated the regulatory roles of TCP genes during leaf development.
- Built practical experience in genetics, molecular biology, and experimental research.

## Research context

This project was supervised by **Prof. Genji Qin** at Peking University.
